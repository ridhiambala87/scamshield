How ScamShield AI Works

The Hybrid Threat Intelligence Pipeline: combining deterministic heuristic signals with Groq AI contextual reasoning.

1

Multi-Modal Ingestion

ScamShield accepts text, uploaded screenshots (via local Tesseract OCR with manual correction support), raw URLs, or QR codes (decoded via jsQR into URLs or UPI deep-links).

2

Deterministic Heuristic Rule Engine

Evaluates known threat signatures across 6 core vectors: urgency coercion, authority/bank impersonation, credential harvesting (OTP/PIN/Pass), payment triggers, job/investment scams, and disposable TLDs.

3

URL & Domain Reputation Inspection

Dissects domain structures, detects lookalike typosquatting against major institutions (SBI, HDFC, Amazon), inspects IP-based hosts, excessive subdomain chaining, and unencrypted HTTP transport.

4

Groq AI Contextual Reasoning Engine

Groq's high-speed inference model analyzes intent, psychological manipulation tactics, social engineering subtleties, and multi-lingual nuances while operating within strict prompt injection boundaries.

5

Hybrid Risk Aggregation & Scoring

Combines rule points and AI contextual evaluation into an explainable 0–100 composite score with full transparent point attribution.

6

Actionable Protection & Family Mode

Generates prioritized defense steps, emergency 1930 recovery workflows, multi-level explanations (Simple, Technical, Family), and one-click WhatsApp safety warning cards.
